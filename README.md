# Inventory-Analysis-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing inventory performance across 104 SKUs, 5 product categories, and 15,000 records from 2020–2021. Covers demand trends, ABC classification, profit contribution, and inventory turnover—designed for demand planning insights and decision-making.


# 📦 Inventory Performance Dashboard (2020–2021)

This Power BI dashboard evaluates inventory performance across 104 SKUs over two years. It provides a comprehensive view of sales, profit, inventory turnover, ABC classification, and category trends to support strategic demand planning decisions.

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

- **Rows**: 15,000
- **Tables**: 5
- **Distinct SKUs**: 104
- **Product Categories**: 5
  - Home Accessories
  - Decoration
  - Toys & Edibles
  - Jewelry
  - Office & School
- **Time Period**: 2020–2021
- **Metrics Captured**:
  - Units Sold
  - Revenue
  - Profit
  - COGS
  - Inventory Turnover
  - Remaining Stock
  - ABC Classification (A = High Value, B = Medium, C = Low Class)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Overall Performance (2020 vs 2021)
- 📉 Revenue dropped from **$599K** (2020) to **$467K** (2021) (-21.96%)
- 📦 Quantity sold decreased by 15%
- 💰 COGS dropped by **23.1%**, improving margins
- 🧮 Total Profit:  
  - 2020: **$0.26M**  
  - 2021: **$0.20M**

### 2. Category & ABC Insights
- Most revenue in 2020 came from **Home Accessories** and **Decoration**
- **High-value (A class)** SKUs: dominated by Home Accessories and Decoration
- **Low turnover** SKUs cluster in Jewelry and Office & School categories
- Revenue and profit declined across most categories, especially **Decoration**

### 3. Inventory Turnover Analysis
- Average turnover: **0.94**
- Several SKUs show **< 1.0 turnover**, suggesting overstock
- Best-performing SKU turnover: **1.36**
- Inventory Turnover by Category:
  - 📌 Jewelry: 0.29
  - 🏠 Home Accessories: 1.00
  - ✨ Decoration: 0.99

---

## 📍 Strategic Insights for Demand Planning

- 🧠 **Focus Forecasting on A-Class SKUs**: These drive the majority of revenue and profit.
- 📦 **Reduce Overstock Risk**: Low-turnover products in categories like Jewelry need tighter control.
- 🌍 **Optimize by Region**: Certain areas underperform—target promotions or demand shifts regionally.
- 📉 **Plan for Decline in Demand**: Both revenue and units sold dropped YoY, suggesting demand softening.
- 🏷️ **Improve Product Mix**: Some low-class SKUs consume inventory space without contributing to profit.
